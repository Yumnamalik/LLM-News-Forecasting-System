## Project Discussion

The supervisors discussed the relationship between the **Ontology** and **Knowledge Graph** components of the project.

It was decided that the ontology and knowledge graph would be developed as a unified part of the project rather than being treated as completely separate project components.

The main focus of the project will be **News Forecasting** supported by the ontology and knowledge graph.

### Domain Group 1

**Yumna Aziz & Hamna Tariq**

### Domain Group 2

**Noor Fatima & Aqsa Bibi**

## Key Decisions

The following decisions were made during the meeting:

1. The two existing groups would be merged into one four-member Final Year Project team.
2. The complete team will work on the **LLM-Integrated News Forecasting System Using Ontology & Knowledge Graph**.
3. Ontology and Knowledge Graph will be developed as an integrated part of the project.
